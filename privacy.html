Privacy Policy

Last updated · prototype draft

This explains what Baskin Success collects and how it is used. It is a campus service run by UC Santa Cruz.

What we collect

Your name and ucsc.edu email from Google sign-in; the events you RSVP to and check in at; your points balance and reward entries; and basic device information used to keep check-ins honest.

How we use it

To show you relevant events, record check-ins and points, run rewards and raffles, send the alerts you have turned on, and detect duplicate or automated check-ins. Aggregate, de-identified numbers help the university understand event attendance.

What other people see

Your name appears on event attendee lists and the leaderboard. Organizations see check-in counts and, for their own events, who checked in.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information is shared with campus staff who administer Baskin Success and with reward partners only as needed to fulfill a prize you entered. We may disclose information if required by law or university policy.

Retention

Account and activity data is kept while your account is active and for a limited period afterward for reporting and dispute resolution, then deleted or de-identified.

Your choices

You can edit alert preferences and privacy toggles in Settings, and request account deletion. Deletion removes your profile and disassociates your activity from you; some aggregate counts remain.

To ask about your data or request deletion, contact the Baskin Success team.
